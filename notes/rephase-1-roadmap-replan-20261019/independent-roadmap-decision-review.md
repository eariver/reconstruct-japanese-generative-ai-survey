The still-unclosed contradictions in the correction draft are:

1. **Unchanged strict Longform receipt context vs old authored archive vs new reader.** `validate_receipt:665–758` binds reviewed reader input, immutable authored archive (`publication-semantic-input`), accepted refs, and canonical `survey_root` outputs through `load_derivation_for_readback` with FILE-byte replay. 2. **No external semantic+visual inputs in `finish`.** `finish` generates manuscript/bundle/SEMANTIC_EDITORIAL/VISUAL/Gate/receipt versioned-first from the just-installed live bytes and then validates them against those same bytes. The genuine external review inputs (independent semantic/visual judgments of the exact source/PDF pair, Human Preview decision) are absent from the operation; self-generated QA validated against its own source is a generated-PASS loop of exactly the kind decision §3 rejects. Existing `build_review_record` overwrite-refusal and check-family/fidelity rules do not cure the missing independent reviewer.
3. **In-memory recovery authority.** The bounded `_EditorialInstallBasis` context is held in memory across `prepare/install/finish`, spanning the explicitly acknowledged `TRANSITION_INVALID_LIVE` window. Crash/power-loss inside the window destroys the only record of expected-old/new digests, owned-temp identities, and per-file dispositions; retained COPY + staged bytes + digests on disk do not reconstruct the transaction's intent without a persisted, crash-safe transaction log, which the draft does not define.
